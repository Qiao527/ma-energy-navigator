Electric Delivery Rates
The delivery portion of your bill includes the costs of maintaining and upgrading the grid, as well as funding for critical customer services and programs, taxes and other state and federally mandated charges.
Learn what these charges fund.
Current Rates
Delivery rates change twice each year – on January 1 and July 1. Rates are subject to change at the discretion of the Massachusetts Department of Public Utilities (DPU).
The below rates are the most common for residential customers. You may have a different rate.
View summary of electric rates in Massachusetts.
