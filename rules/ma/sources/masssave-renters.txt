Energy Aligned Lease
What is an Energy Aligned Lease?
An Energy Aligned Lease Clause, also known as a Green Lease, is a rental contract that includes measures that increase the efficiency of the building to meet sustainability goals, improve the health and safety for tenant or those who use the space, and provide financial savings/benefits to both landlord/owner and tenant. Energy Aligned Leases often look like traditional leases with additional clauses detailing financial incentives of sustainability initiatives for both the landlord and the tenant.
These types of leases establish and fulfill energy efficiency goals while assigning financial responsibility in a way that benefits both the renter and the landlord.
What Do Energy Aligned Leases Include?
Energy Aligned Leases include what the sustainability goals are, what needs to be done to reach these goals, how the tenant and landlord will work together to implement the measures, etc.
What Measures Can Energy Aligned Leases Cover?
Common measures outlined in Energy Aligned Leases include indoor air quality requirements, HVAC maintenance schedule/requirements, requiring potential lease sites to have specified provisions integrated before leasing, requirements for collecting and sharing energy usage data, protocols for mechanical upgrades, etc.
