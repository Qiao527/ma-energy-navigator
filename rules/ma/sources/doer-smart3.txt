This page contains information on the current version of the Commonwealth's SMART solar incentive program (225 CMR 28.00) for solar installers and developers. On this page you will find resources to participate in SMART, current incentive rates and capacity, program implementation guidelines, and regulatory updates.

Program Regulations
September 2026 Regulations Filed
On
September 11, 2026
, the Department of Energy Resources (DOER) filed final revised regulations with the Secretary of State's Office for the SMART 3.0 Program under 225 CMR 28.00. DOER expects the final regulations to publish in the Massachusetts Register on
September 25, 2026
.
DOER filed
emergency regulations for 225 CMR 28.00
on
June 26, 2026
. DOER held a virtual public hearing on the emergency regulations on
August 7, 2026
, and received 15 sets of public comments.
DOER made several clerical and administrative fixes along with minor eligibility and process clarifications to improve program implementation. In response to public comment, DOER added a definition for a Pre-paid Solar Lease and provided additional information on how such an offering will satisfy the Third-party Owned Savings Requirement in forthcoming changes to the Consumer Protection Guideline. Additionally, DOER clarified the Guaranteed Discount Requirements for Municipal Load Aggregation Programs and Electric Distribution Company CSS Programs.
The final 225 CMR 28.00 regulations as filed are linked below.

Program Year Annual Capacity and Rates
Per program regulations, DOER will publish an Annual Report with details on available capacity, set asides, and incentive rates for the applicable Program Year. In a given Program Year, DOER will accept applications from January 1 through December 31 of that year. Reports and implementation materials from previous program years can be found on the Program Year Reports and Annual Assessments webpage.

Program Year 2026 Flat Incentive Rates (≤25 kW)

Unlike other STGU types, these incentive rates are fixed for all STGUs of this kind, based on the previous year's average
net metering credit value

Program Year 2026 BTM Value of Energy Workbook
DOER has created a Value of Energy and Incentive Calculator for Behind-the-Meter facilities >25 kW AC. This calculator is intended to be a tool to calculate the Value of Energy and fixed incentive compensation rate for Behind-the-Meter systems. The calculation is based on project type, size, distribution company service territory, and customer rate class. This workbook does not necessarily reflect the final tariff value a system will be qualified for, but is available as a tool to help estimate the value that a particular customer may receive under different scenarios. This workbook will be in effect through December 31, 2026.
